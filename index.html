The basics

How to play

Simple to learn. Much harder to master. The rules are all about keeping your finger down and dodging danger.

01

Start your escape

Tap the start prompt, then touch and hold the screen when you're ready to begin.

02

Dodge everything

Drag your finger to avoid incoming monsters and obstacles. Touching one ends your run.

03

Don't let go!

Keep your finger on the screen. Lift it, and it's game over. Survive longer to discover new worlds.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Check these common questions first. Still need help? Send us an email and tell us what happened.

Why does my run end when I lift my finger?
That's part of the challenge! Once your run begins, you need to keep your finger on the screen. Letting go or touching an enemy or obstacle causes a game over.
How do I reach new worlds?
Keep surviving. As your score rises, the game introduces new backgrounds, enemies, movement patterns, and tougher challenges.
Where is my best score saved?
Your personal best is saved on your device. Removing the app or resetting its local data may remove your saved score.
What if I can't hear the music or sound effects?
Check your device volume and sound settings. If the problem continues, email us with your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.
How do I report a bug or crash?
Email noteyappil@gmail.com with what happened, when it happened, your iPhone model, and your iOS version. A screenshot or short screen recording can help us investigate.
